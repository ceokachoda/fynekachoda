# FyneStudy — Student Guide (Mobile App)

Welcome to FyneStudy! This is your complete, step-by-step guide to the FyneStudy mobile app. It is written for students (and parents) who are new to the app — no technical knowledge needed. Read it top to bottom the first time, then keep it handy as a reference.

> **Other guides:** Teachers should read the [Teacher Guide](./teacher-guide.md). Institute staff and owners should read the [Admin Guide](./admin-guide.md).

**What you can do in the app:**
- See your personal dashboard (streak, rank, weak topics, today's classes)
- Mark your attendance by showing a QR code to your teacher
- Browse the study Library — watch videos and read PDF notes
- Take practice quizzes and graded exams
- Join live classes (with chat and "raise hand") and watch recordings
- See your rank on the leaderboard and collect badges
- Track your topic mastery and view your profile

## 1. Introduction

### Where does my account come from?

You **cannot sign up by yourself.** There is no "Create account" or "Register" button anywhere in FyneStudy — and that is on purpose. Your institute (your coaching centre) creates your account for you and assigns you to a **batch** (your class group). Your batch decides which **course** you're in (for example JEE, NEET, or CUET).

When your account is created, the institute sends you an email with:
- Your **login email** (usually the same email you gave the institute)
- A **temporary password** to use the first time only

Keep that email handy — you'll need it in section 2.

### How do I install the app?

The bottom navigation bar (the row of icons at the bottom of the screen) is your main way to move around the app. You'll see **6 tabs**:

| Tab | Icon | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | house | Your dashboard — streak, rank, today's plan |
| **Classes** | video | Live classes, recordings, and your exams |
| **Library** | open book | Study videos, PDF notes, and topic quizzes |
| **Attendance** | QR code | Show your QR to get marked present |
| **Ranks** | trophy | The batch leaderboard |
| **Profile** | person | Your details, mastery, badges, settings, and Sign out |

To get the app on your phone, ask your institute which option applies to you:

| How you got the app | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Expo Go (testing / early access)** | Install the free **Expo Go** app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android), then open the link or scan the QR code your institute shares with you. |
| **App download link (APK / TestFlight)** | Tap the link your institute sends and follow your phone's install prompts. |
| **App Store / Google Play (public release)** | Search for **FyneStudy** in the store and tap Install. |

The app works fully on **both iPhone and Android**. It is built to run smoothly even on older, low-cost Android phones.

> **Camera permission:** When you scan a QR (you only do this if you're a teacher) the app asks for camera access. As a student you mainly **show** your QR, so you usually won't need the camera — but it's safe to allow it if asked.

## 2. Getting started

### Step 1 — Open the app

When the app opens for the first time you'll see the **Welcome** screen with the FyneStudy logo and the tagline *"Best Teachers · Live Classes · Mock Tests · Notes."* At the bottom it reminds you: *"Admin-issued accounts only. Contact your institute if you don't have one."*

### Step 2 — Sign in

1. In the **Email** box, type the email address your institute registered for you.
2. In the **Password** box, type the **temporary password** from your welcome email exactly as written (passwords are case-sensitive).
3. Tap the blue **Sign in** button.
   - The button shows **"Signing in…"** while it works.
   - If something is wrong, a red message appears under the boxes (for example "Invalid login credentials"). Double-check the email and password and try again.

### Step 3 — Choose your own password (first login only)

The very first time you sign in, the app takes you to a **"Choose a new password"** screen. This is required — you can't continue until you set your own password.

1. In **New password**, type a password that follows these rules:
   - At least **10 characters**
   - Includes an **uppercase** letter, a **lowercase** letter, and a **digit**
   - **No spaces**
   - It **cannot be the same as your email**
2. In **Confirm new password**, type the exact same password again.
3. Tap **Save and continue**.
   - If the two passwords don't match, you'll see *"Passwords do not match."*
   - If your password is too weak, you'll see a message telling you what's missing.

> **Tip:** Pick something you'll remember but others can't guess. Write it somewhere safe. You'll use this password every time from now on.

Once saved, the app takes you straight to your **Home** dashboard. You're in!

### A tour of the Home dashboard

Home is the screen you land on every time you open the app. From top to bottom:

| Item | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Greeting** | "Good morning/afternoon/evening, [your name]." It changes with the time of day. |
| **Streak flame** (top-right) | A flame icon with a number — your current **daily streak** (days in a row you've been active). Tap it to open the full streak view. The flame changes colour as your streak grows. |
| **"Up next" card** (blue) | The single most important thing for you right now — a live class to join, an exam window that's open, a class starting soon, attendance to mark, a new recording, or a weak topic to practise. Tap the button on the card to jump straight there. |
| **Stats strip** (3 pills) | **Attendance %**, **Mastery %**, and your **Rank**. Tap any pill to open that section (Attendance, your Mastery tab, or the Leaderboard). |
| **Today's schedule** | Your classes for today, with the status (Present / Late / Absent or upcoming). |
| **Weak topics** | Topics where your score is low. Each shows your mastery % and a "Practice this topic" link. Tap one to start a quiz (or open the library). If you're doing great everywhere, it says *"You're on top of every topic."* |
| **Continue watching** | Videos you started but didn't finish — tap to pick up where you left off. (Only shows when you have unfinished videos.) |
| **Recent badges** | The latest achievement badges you've unlocked. |

**To refresh Home**, pull down from the top of the screen and let go. The dashboard reloads with the newest info. It also refreshes automatically each time you come back to the tab.

## 3. BASIC

This section covers the three things you'll do most often: marking attendance, browsing the library, and checking your profile.

### 3.1 Mark your attendance

Your teacher marks attendance by scanning a QR code that **your** phone shows. The QR is unique to you and changes every few seconds for security, so a screenshot won't work — you must show it live.

**Steps:**

1. Tap the **Attendance** tab (QR-code icon) at the bottom.
2. At the top you'll see the **Attendance** title and *"Show your QR to the teacher."*
3. If a class is open for attendance right now, a large white card appears with your **QR code** in the middle.
   - Just below the QR it says **"Refreshes in 25s"** (or similar) and counts down. This is normal — the code automatically rotates roughly every **25–30 seconds** so it stays secure. You don't need to do anything; keep the screen showing.
4. **Hold your phone up to the teacher** (or to the classroom scanner) so they can scan the QR.
5. The moment you're scanned, your card turns into a green tick with **"You're marked!"** — you're done.

**Below the QR you'll also see:**

- **Today's classes** — a list of your classes for today. Each one has a coloured status tag:

  | Tag | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | **Scan now** (blue) | Attendance is open — show your QR. |
  | **Upcoming** (grey) | Class hasn't started yet. |
  | **Present** (green) | You've been marked present. |
  | **Late** (amber) | You were marked, but after the on-time window. |
  | **Absent** (red) | You were not marked for this class. |
  | **Closed** (grey) | The attendance window has ended. |

  If you have **more than one** class open at once, tap a class in the list (or one of the small pills under the QR) to switch which class your QR is for.

- **My history** — two rings showing your attendance **This week** and over the **Last 30 days**, plus a list of your recent classes and how you were marked.

**If the QR won't appear** you might see a friendly message instead — for example:
- *"You're already marked for this class."* (Great — nothing more to do.)
- *"Scan window is closed."* (Attendance for that class has ended.)
- *"This class isn't in your batch."* (You're looking at a class that isn't yours.)
- A network message — tap **Try again** once your internet is back.

### 3.2 Browse the Library and open a video or PDF

The Library holds all your study materials, organised so they're easy to find.

**Steps:**

1. Tap the **Library** tab (open-book icon).
2. You start at the **Subjects** screen — a grid of subject cards (for example Physics, Chemistry, Maths). Each card shows how many items it has.
3. Tap a subject → you see its **Chapters**.
4. Tap a chapter → you see its **Topics**.
5. Tap a topic → you see the **Items** inside: videos, PDFs/notes, and any **Practice Quizzes** for that topic (listed at the bottom).

To go **back** one level at any time, tap the round back-arrow button in the top-left corner.

**Search:** On the Subjects screen there's a **"Search library…"** box at the top. Type a word (like "kinematics") to filter the library to matching items.

**Item types** are colour-coded by their icon:

| Icon colour | Type | Opens as |
|---|---|---|
| Red play-circle | **Video** | The video player |
| Green file | **PDF** | The PDF reader |
| Amber sticky-note | **Note** | The PDF/notes reader |

#### Watching a video

1. Tap a video item. It opens the **video player** with the video title at the top.
2. If you've watched part of it before, a panel slides up: **"Resume from [time]?"**
   - Tap **Resume** to continue where you stopped.
   - Tap **Start over** to begin from the beginning.
3. Tap the play button on the video to start. (You'll see a faint watermark with your details on the video — this is normal and protects the institute's content.)
4. **Rotate your phone sideways** to watch full-screen. Rotate back to return to normal view.
5. Below the video you'll see the description and your last-watched position (e.g. *"Last position: 4:12 (35% watched)"*).
6. Tap the back-arrow (top-left) to leave. The video pauses automatically.

> Your progress is saved as you watch, so "Continue watching" on Home and the resume prompt always know where you stopped.

#### Reading a PDF / notes

1. Tap a PDF or note item. It opens the **PDF reader** (dark background) with the title and **"Page X of Y"** at the top.
2. **Scroll up and down** to read. **Pinch** with two fingers to zoom in and out.
3. A faint watermark with your name shows on the page (content protection — this is expected).
4. The reader remembers your page, so next time you open it you're back where you left off.
5. Tap the back-arrow (top-left) to close.

> **Note for Android users:** Screenshots are blocked inside the PDF reader to protect study material. That's intentional.

### 3.3 View your Profile

The **Profile** tab is your personal hub. Tap the **Profile** tab (person icon).

At the top is your **profile card** — your initials, your full name, and your email.

Just below are **three sub-tabs**: **Profile**, **Mastery**, **Badges**. The **Profile** sub-tab (the default) shows:

- **Account** — your **Full name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Date of birth**. Each has a small lock icon because these are **read-only** (see note below).
- **Academic** — your **Batch** and **Course**, also read-only.
- **Actions:**
  - **Change password** — pick a new password any time.
  - **Contact admin** — opens your email app addressed to the institute, for help, profile updates, or batch changes.
- **Sign out** — a red button to log out (see [section 6](#6-settings--account)).

> **Why are my details locked?** Your name, email, phone, date of birth, batch, and course can only be changed by your institute — not by you. This keeps records accurate. If anything is wrong, tap **Contact admin** and ask them to fix it.

(We'll cover the **Mastery** and **Badges** sub-tabs in the [Advanced](#5-advanced) section.)

## 4. INTERMEDIATE

Now that you know your way around, let's do the core learning activities: quizzes, live classes, and recordings.

### 4.1 Take a practice quiz

Practice quizzes are low-pressure: you can take them whenever you like, see the answers afterwards, and **retake** them as many times as you want. They live inside the Library (at the bottom of a **topic**) and also appear in your **Weak topics** on Home.

**How to start:**

1. Open a quiz — either from a topic in the **Library** (under "Practice Quizzes"), from a **Weak topics** card on Home, or from the blue "Up next" card.
2. You land on the **intro screen** showing:
   - The quiz **title**
   - How many **questions** and the **time limit** (e.g. *"10 questions · 15 min"*)
   - The **marking scheme**: marks for a **Correct** answer, a **Wrong** answer, and a **Skip**, plus the **Total possible** score.
3. Tap the blue **Start Quiz** button.

> It says *"Auto-saves every action. You can leave and come back."* — true! If you close the app mid-quiz, your answers are saved and you resume where you left off.

**Answering questions:**

- The question shows at the top with its number (e.g. **Q1 / 10**) and a difficulty tag (EASY / MEDIUM / HARD).
- Tap an option (**A, B, C, …**) to select it. The selected option highlights.
- Made a mistake? Tap **Clear** (next to the flag) to unselect, or just tap a different option.

**Math questions:** Some questions and options contain math formulas (square roots, fractions, exponents, etc.). These render as proper, nicely-typeset math automatically — you don't need to do anything. Images attached to a question also show below the question text.

**Flagging & the navigation grid:**

- Not sure about a question? Tap the **flag** button to mark it for review. Flagged questions are easy to find later.
- Near the bottom is the **navigation grid** — a row of numbered squares, one per question. Their colour tells you the status:

  | Colour | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Blue (highlighted) | The question you're on now |
  | Green / filled | Answered |
  | Flagged style | Flagged for review (answered or not) |
  | Plain / empty | Not answered yet |

  Tap any number to **jump** straight to that question.
- Use **Prev** and **Next** at the bottom to move one question at a time.

**The timer:** A countdown pill is at the top-right. When time runs out the quiz **submits automatically** so you never lose your work.

**Submitting:**

1. On the last question (or any time the timer ends), tap the green **Submit** button.
2. A confirmation pops up: *"Submit quiz? You've answered X/Y…"*. Tap **Submit** to confirm or **Cancel** to keep going.

**Your result:**

After submitting you see the **Result** screen:
- Your **score** out of the total, and your **percentage**.
- A breakdown: **Correct**, **Wrong**, **Skipped**.

From here you can:
- **View Solutions** — read the correct answer and explanation for every question. (Math and images show here too.)
- **Retake** — start the quiz over from the intro screen.
- **Back to Library** — return to where you came from.

> **Good to know:** During a quiz the app never tells you whether an answer is right — you only find that out in **Solutions** after you submit. This keeps practice honest.

### 4.2 Join a LIVE class

When your teacher goes live, you can watch the stream and chat in real time.

**Steps:**

1. Tap the **Classes** tab (video icon).
2. At the top are three segments: **Live**, **Upcoming**, **Recorded**. Tap **Live**.
   - A class that's on air shows a red **"Live"** tag.
   - If nothing is live, you'll see *"No classes are live right now."*
   - (You can also tap the **"Join Live Now"** button on the Home "Up next" card.)
3. Tap the live class to open it.

**Inside a live class:**

- **Before the teacher starts the video**, you'll see a **lobby countdown** — the class title and a timer counting down to start. Hang tight; the video appears automatically when the teacher goes live.
- Once live, the **video player** plays the stream at the top (with your watermark). A red **LIVE** badge shows in the header.
- **Chat** fills the area below the video:
  - Read messages from your teacher and classmates as they arrive.
  - Type in the box at the bottom and send to join the conversation. (There's a gentle limit on how fast you can send, to keep chat readable.)
  - If your teacher **pins** an announcement, it shows in a banner above the chat.
- **Raise your hand:** Tap the **Raise hand** button to let the teacher know you have a question. It shows **"Hand raised ✋"**. Tap again to lower it.
- **Full-screen:** Rotate your phone sideways for a bigger view of the stream.

**When the class ends**, the screen shows **"This class has ended."** If the recording is ready, tap **Watch recording** to view it. Otherwise the recording will appear under the **Recorded** segment once it's processed.

> **If the chat says you've been muted:** A teacher can mute a student in chat. You can still **read** the chat but can't send messages until they un-mute you.

### 4.3 Watch a recorded class

Missed a live class, or want to revise? Recordings include the original chat played back in sync.

**Steps:**

1. Tap the **Classes** tab → tap the **Recorded** segment. (Recordings also appear in your **Today's schedule** with a purple **"Watch"** tag.)
2. Tap a recording to open it.
3. It opens the **recording player** with a **RECORDING** tag at the top.

**Controls (in the bar below the video):**
- **Play / Pause** button (round blue button on the left).
- A **progress bar** you can drag to scrub to any point.
- The current time and total length on either side.
- **Speed** buttons: **1×**, **1.5×**, **2×** — tap to speed up or slow down playback.

**Synced chat replay:** Below the controls, the chat from the original live class **replays in time** with the video — as you scrub or change speed, the messages keep pace. It's like re-living the class.

Rotate sideways for full-screen. Tap the back-arrow (top-left) to leave.

> If you open a recording very soon after a class ends, you might see *"This recording is still being processed by YouTube. Check back shortly."* — just wait a little and try again.

## 5. ADVANCED

This section covers graded exams, the leaderboard, badges, streaks, and your mastery — the features that track your serious progress.

### 5.1 Take a graded EXAM

Exams are **serious, timed tests**. Unlike quizzes, the **server controls the clock** (your phone's clock can't change it), the app **records if you leave the app** during the exam, and you usually take an exam **once**. Read this section before your first exam.

**Where to find exams:**
- Tap the **Classes** tab and scroll to the **Exams** section, or
- Tap an exam from the **"Up next"** card on Home.

Each exam in the list shows its title, start time, duration, and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | Hasn't started yet. |
| **Live now** (red) | The exam window is open — you can enter. |
| **Ended** | The window has closed. |
| **Submitted · awaiting release** | You've finished; waiting for your teacher to release results. |
| **Score X/Y** (green) | Results are out — tap to see them. |

**Before the exam (the "Rules" screen):**

Tap the exam to open it. You'll see the title, the start time, duration and number of questions, and a **Rules** box that says:
- *Server clock decides. You can't change your phone clock to extend time.*
- *Leaving the app is **logged** (a counter your teacher can see).*
- *Auto-saves as you answer. If you re-open, you resume in place.*
- *Results are shown immediately on submit* **or** *released by your teacher* (depends on the exam).

- If the exam hasn't started, you'll see **"Starts in"** with a countdown, and the **Enter Exam** button is greyed out.
- When the window opens it shows a red **"Live now"**, how long until the window closes, and an active blue **Enter Exam** button.

**Taking the exam:**

Tap **Enter Exam.** The exam screen looks like a quiz (question card, options A/B/C…, flag, navigation grid, Prev/Next) with two important differences:

1. **The timer (top-right)** counts down your time. It stays accurate even if you change your phone's clock — the institute's server is the real timekeeper.
2. **The tab-switch banner.** If you leave the FyneStudy app during the exam (switch apps, answer a call, go to your home screen) and come back, a banner appears showing how many times you've left. **Your teacher sees this count.** Stay in the app for the whole exam.

Answer exactly like a quiz: tap options, flag questions, jump around with the grid. Everything **auto-saves**, so if your phone dies or you get disconnected, re-open the exam and you'll resume right where you were.

**Submitting:**

- Tap **Submit** on the last question (or anytime), then confirm in the pop-up (*"Submit exam? … This cannot be undone."*).
- **If you run out of time**, the exam **auto-submits** for you — you won't lose anything.

**Getting your results — two cases:**

| Exam type | What happens after you submit |
|---|---|
| **Instant results** | Your **score appears immediately** — score, percentage, and the Correct/Wrong/Skipped breakdown. |
| **Teacher-released results** | You see a **"Submitted"** screen: *"Results will be released by your teacher."* Check back later, or tap **Check release status** to refresh. Once your teacher releases them, the screen flips to your results automatically. |

When results are available, tap **View Solutions** to see the correct answer and explanation for each question.

> **Re-opening a finished exam:** If you tap an exam you've already submitted, the app takes you straight to your results (instant exams) or the "waiting for release" screen (teacher-released exams).

### 5.2 The Leaderboard (Ranks)

The leaderboard shows how you rank against the **other students in your batch**. It's friendly competition to keep you motivated.

**Steps:**

1. Tap the **Ranks** tab (trophy icon).
2. At the top it shows your **batch name** and a big blue card with **"Your rank"** — your position (e.g. **#3 / 24**) and your **composite** score.
3. Below is the ranked list of your batch. Your own row is highlighted so you can spot yourself.

**Scope tabs** (near the top) let you switch the time window — for example **Weekly** vs **All-time**. Tap a scope to change which period you're ranked over.

**How is my rank calculated?** Scroll to the bottom and tap **"How is this calculated?"** A panel explains it. Your **composite score** (0 to 1) blends three things:

| Weight | Part | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **60%** | Quiz & exam scores | Your average score across quizzes and exams in the period. |
| **25%** | Activity | How many days you were active out of the days you could be. |
| **15%** | Streak | Your current daily streak (full credit at a 30-day streak). |

Ties are broken by quiz/exam score, then number of quizzes taken, then name. **Everyone is ranked the same fair way — and only you can see your own scores** (you can't see classmates' exact numbers).

> **The takeaway:** to climb the board, keep your scores up, stay active most days, and keep your streak alive.

**Tap a classmate's row** to see their **public card** — their name, batch, current streak, and the badges they've earned. (It only shows things that are meant to be public; never their private scores.) Tap **Close** to dismiss it.

If the board is empty it says *"No rankings yet"* with a nudge to take a quiz, mark attendance, or keep your streak going.

### 5.3 Badges & streaks

#### Badges

Badges are achievement medals you unlock by reaching milestones. See your whole collection on the **Profile** tab → **Badges** sub-tab.

- Earned badges show in **full colour**; locked ones are **dimmed with a small lock**.
- At the top it shows e.g. *"4 of 11 earned."*
- **Tap any badge** to see details — for an earned one, the date you got it; for a locked one, **how to earn it**.

Here are all **11 badges** and how to earn each:

| Badge | How to earn it |
|---|---|
| **First Step** | Submit your first practice quiz |
| **Week Warrior** | Reach a 7-day active streak |
| **Marathoner** | Reach a 30-day active streak |
| **Iron Mind** | Reach a 90-day active streak |
| **Showed Up** | 100% attendance across 7 consecutive class days |
| **Top of the Class** | Finish #1 on your weekly batch leaderboard |
| **So Close** | Finish #2 or #3 on your weekly batch leaderboard |
| **Centurion** | Submit 100 practice quizzes |
| **Subject Specialist** | Average 80%+ mastery across a subject (at least 3 topics) |
| **Early Bird** | Scan in before the scheduled start time 5 times |
| **Comeback** | Rebuild a 7-day streak after losing a 7+ day streak |

**The celebration pop-up:** When you earn a new badge, the next time you open Home a **"Badge unlocked!"** pop-up appears with confetti, the badge, and its name. Tap **Awesome!** to dismiss it. (If you earn several at once, they show one after another.)

#### Streaks

Your **streak** is the number of days **in a row** you've been active in FyneStudy. Tap the **flame** on Home (top-right) — or open the streak view — to see it in detail:

- A big flame and your **current streak**, plus your **best streak** ever.
- A **"Last 30 days"** calendar grid: **green** squares are days you were active, **grey** squares are days you weren't, and today has a **blue outline**.
- Any badges you earned during the current streak.

**How to keep your streak alive:** do **at least one** of these each day —
- mark attendance,
- finish a quiz or exam, or
- watch a video to at least the halfway point.

Miss a whole day and the streak **resets to zero**. All days are counted in **IST** (Indian Standard Time). The flame also changes colour as your streak grows (grey → orange → red → gold), so longer streaks look hotter.

### 5.4 Your mastery

**Mastery** shows how well you're doing in each topic, so you know what to revise.

1. Tap the **Profile** tab → the **Mastery** sub-tab (or tap the **Mastery %** pill on Home).
2. You'll see your topics listed **weakest first**, each with a mastery percentage.
3. Mastery is the **rolling average of your last 5 attempts** in each topic — counting both quizzes **and** exams. So the more recent practice you do, the more your mastery reflects your current level.

If you haven't taken anything yet it says *"No mastery data yet"* — finish a quiz or exam and your mastery appears here. Use this list together with the **Weak topics** card on Home to decide what to practise next.

## 6. Settings & account

### Where settings live

The **Profile** tab (person icon) is your one-stop place for everything account-related — it shows your details, mastery, and badges, and it's where you'll find the **Change password**, **Contact admin**, and **Sign out** actions.

Open the **Profile** tab → scroll to the bottom → tap the red **Sign out** button. You'll be returned to the **Sign in** screen. You'll need your email and password to get back in, so make sure you remember the password you chose.

### What you can and can't change yourself

| You CAN change yourself | Only the INSTITUTE can change |

| | Your **date of birth** |
| | Your **batch** and **course** |

## 7. FAQ & troubleshooting

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
On the **Sign in** screen, tap **"Forgot password?"**. Enter your registered email and tap **Send reset link** — the institute's system emails you a one-time reset link that expires in **1 hour**. Open it from your phone and set a new password. If you don't get the email, check spam, or **contact your institute** to reset it for you.

**Q: I can't log in / "Invalid login credentials."**
- Make sure your **email** is typed correctly (no extra spaces) and your **password** is exactly right (it's case-sensitive).
- If you've never logged in, use the **temporary password** from your welcome email.
- Still stuck? Use **Forgot password?**, or contact your institute — only they can re-issue your account.

**Q: There's no "Sign up" button. How do I create an account?**
You don't — and you can't. Accounts are created **only** by your institute. If you don't have one yet, contact them.

**Q: My QR won't scan / no QR appears on the Attendance tab.**
- A QR only shows when a class is **open** for attendance right now. If you see a status like **Upcoming**, **Closed**, or **You're already marked**, that explains it.
- Keep the screen bright and steady while the teacher scans; the code rotates every ~25–30 seconds, which is normal.
- If you see *"This class isn't in your batch,"* you're on a class that isn't yours.
- On a network error, tap **Try again** once your internet is back.

**Q: A video or PDF won't open ("Couldn't authorize playback" / "Couldn't load PDF").**
This is almost always a temporary connection issue. Tap **Go back**, check your internet, and re-open the item. If it keeps failing, the item may have been removed by the institute.

**Q: The live class is just a countdown / blank.**
That's the **lobby** — the teacher hasn't started the stream yet. The video appears automatically once they go live. If it's been a while, pull to refresh the Classes tab, or leave and re-open the class.

**Q: I submitted my exam but don't see a score.**
Some exams show results instantly; others are **released by your teacher** later. If you see *"Results will be released by your teacher,"* check back after the teacher releases them, or tap **Check release status**.

**Q: My rank, mastery, or streak looks out of date.**
Pull down to refresh the screen. Some figures (like leaderboard rankings) update on a regular schedule rather than instantly, so they may take a little while to catch up after you finish an activity.

**Q: Can my classmates see my scores?**
No. On the leaderboard everyone sees the **ranking order**, but **only you** can see your own exact scores. A classmate's public card only shows their name, batch, streak, and badges.

**Q: Screenshots don't work in the PDF reader.**
That's intentional on Android, to protect the institute's study materials.

**Q: How do I get help?**
On your **Profile** tab, tap **Contact admin** — it opens an email to your institute. For anything about your account, batch, name, or grades, the institute is the right place to ask.
